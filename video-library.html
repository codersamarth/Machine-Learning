Make a video for one lesson

  1. Choose a lesson and download its Notebook source (.md).
  2. Open Gemini Notebook, create a notebook, and upload the downloaded source. Use only that lesson's source to keep the explanation focused.
  3. In Studio → Video Overview, choose Explainer for the complete lesson or Short for a quick recap. Paste the lesson's tailored prompt.
  4. Generate the video, then review its explanation and equations against the lesson. Download the video or share it with access enabled.
  5. Paste an accessible Video Overview link, MP4 URL or YouTube link into the lesson's video slot. It is saved locally on this device.

Videos are generated by Google, not by ML Playground. Their availability, duration, language, generation limits and sharing depend on your Google account. Private notebook links may work only while signed in. The source packs are original study explanations, not copies of restricted course handouts.
