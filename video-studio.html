How to generate and attach a video

1

Download your source

Pick one topic, then download its Markdown source pack. It contains the explanation, code and source labels.

2

Generate in NotebookLM

Add that source to a notebook, open Studio → Video Overview → Explainer and paste the steering prompt. Select a whiteboard style where available.

3

Check accuracy

Verify the math, code and narration against your lecture and the website. AI-generated videos can contain mistakes.

4

Attach your video

Share the video from NotebookLM, then paste its permitted share link here. Saved links appear as “Watch” within this Video Studio.

To publish the same videos to every visitor, add the reviewed links to video-map.js in GitHub. A public notebook can expose its sources; do not publicly share copyrighted university handouts or restricted case materials.
